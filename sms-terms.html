Program Description

W.M. Martin Transport sends conversational SMS messages to carriers, drivers, shippers and receivers about the freight shipments we arrange: dispatch details, load offers and confirmations for a specific shipment, pickup and delivery appointment confirmations, and shipment status updates. We do not send marketing or promotional messages. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of doing business with us.

How You Opt In

You opt in verbally by phone when a load is booked, dispatched or scheduled. Our staff reads this statement and records your consent: “Can W.M. Martin Transport text you about this shipment at this number? Message frequency varies, about 1–2 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Our privacy policy and terms are at wmmartin.com/privacy-policy and wmmartin.com/sms-terms.”

Message Frequency and Rates

Message frequency varies, about 1–2 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier’s plan.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive one confirmation message and no further texts unless you opt in again.
